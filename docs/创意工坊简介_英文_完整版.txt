[h1]The Annal of Han-Jin · 汉晋春秋[/h1]

【Cover image: replace with [img]image URL[/img]】

[quote]The seventh year of Guanghe, 184 AD — a jiazi year. The Han has ruled for nearly four hundred years. In the palace, eunuchs and consort kin fight in the shadows; in the provinces, great families swallow the land while plague and famine drive the people to despair. The Way of Great Peace waits for a single word, and the characters "jiazi" are already chalked on the gates of the capital.
[b]Will you prop up the tottering Han — or ride the storm and remake the world?[/b][/quote]

[b]The Annal of Han-Jin[/b] is a total conversion devoted entirely to East Asia: a rebuilt map, the complete Han bureaucracy, over thirty thousand historical characters, and four centuries of order and chaos — from the Yellow Turbans to the rise of the Sui.

[b]Fully localized in English.[/b] 中文简介：[url=【中文页链接】]汉晋春秋[/url]

[h2]■ The Map[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every Han commandery and county restored: provinces are kingdoms, commanderies are duchies, and over 2,100 counties stretch from the Pamirs and Dayuan to the Mongolian steppe, the Three Han and Wa, and south to Jiaozhi.
[*]Completely remade East Asian terrain art — the most detailed East Asian map on the Workshop.
[*]40+ clothing models and dozens of special buildings in Han-Jin style, plus historical unit models, Chinese coats of arms and redrawn borders.
[*]Map, characters and scripts optimized to run at vanilla speed.
[/list]

[h2]■ The Han Bureaucracy[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Central court:[/b] the Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers; a "Hundred Officials" window shows the whole court at a glance.
[*][b]Provinces:[/b] the Commandery-Kingdom System, Inspectors who may be raised to Governors, and Military Districts granting tallies up to the Borrowed Yellow Axe.
[*][b]Salary ranks[/b] from Ten Thousand Bushels down to Six Hundred Bushels.
[*][b]Paths to office:[/b] recommendation as Filial and Incorrupt or Flourishing Talent, summons, probation and evaluations; Virtuous Repute rises from "Praised in the Village" to "Revered by All Under Heaven".
[*][b]Open Office[/b] with your own staff, and military ranks earned by merit.
[*][b]Imperial house:[/b] empresses and consorts of the inner palace, crown prince and imperial grandson, era names, posthumous and temple names.
[*]In 184, ministers, Inspectors and Administrators follow history wherever possible.
[/list]

[h2]■ The Dynastic Cycle: Eight Eras[/h2]
Order: [b]Rebuilding from Ruin, Expansion, Advancement, Tension, Conquest[/b]. Chaos: [b]Collapse of the Realm, Contest of the Warlords, Tripod Standoff[/b]. What players and AI do pushes the realm toward the next era.
[list]
[*][b]Era decisions and wars:[/b] Light Taxes and Corvee, Grand Amnesty, Edict Seeking Talent; Military Farming, Ennoble for Military Merit, Frontier Expansion; Compile a Great Canon, Revise the Statutes, Reform to Strengthen the State; Proscription of Factions, Restoration Reforms, Purge the Emperor's Side; Sinicization Reforms or Preserve Customs for foreign conquerors; Disaster Relief, Edict to Rescue the Throne, Local Self-Strengthening.
[*][b]Collapse:[/b] mass secession, warlords claiming the throne, great peasant uprisings and crises of authority, alongside floods, earthquakes and famine.
[*][b]Contest of the Warlords:[/b] vassals Demand Independence, former lieges Punish the Rebel, and warlords wage wars of Annexation.
[*][b]Tripod Standoff:[/b] a few great powers face off in wars for the Central Plains. Control 80% of the realm to end the age of chaos; a foreign dynasty that does so begins an era of Conquest.
[*]Regicide, lost defensive wars and failing legitimacy drag the realm into chaos; crushing rebellions and commanding the realm pull it back.
[*]In times of order, only the Son of Heaven may create kingdoms and empires.
[*]The 184 start opens in [b]Tension[/b].
[/list]

[h2]■ Emperor and Warlords[/h2]
[list]
[*]When chaos comes, the emperor's vassals break away, leaving him only the capital and his name.
[*]A strong warlord can [b]Hold the Son of Heaven[/b], become Chancellor and rule in his name: Decree a Campaign, Campaign by Edict, Nominal Governorship, Demand Tribute, Grant Remote Claim.
[*]The emperor fights back: Secretly Win Over outside lords, send a Girdle Edict, Flee, or Purge the Powerful Minister.
[*]Stay [b]Loyal to Han[/b], or [b]Obey Edicts No More[/b] and move toward Force the Abdication and Reduce to a Figurehead. Others may Usurp the Imperial Title — and be met with wars to Punish the Usurper.
[/list]

[h2]■ Court Factions[/h2]
[list]
[*]Imperial courts split into Consort Kin, Eunuchs, Gentry, Humble-Born and Martial Nobles, plus the Four Barbarians.
[*]Each faction takes a stance — loyalist, expansionist, reformist, conservative or neutral — and its leaders contest power in court debates.
[*]Celestial dynasties use house blocs and house unity: houses can ally, pursue aspirations, feud, or split into cadet branches.
[/list]

[h2]■ Characters and Storylines[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]34,000+ characters, 32,000+ of them historical[/b], with lineages and marriages linked from the sources; figures from the Romance appear too.
[*][b]11,000+ historical figures born after 184 arrive at age sixteen[/b], spread through the year, until the end of the Northern and Southern Dynasties. Zhuge Liang, Zhou Yu and Sun Quan grow into their historical talents.
[*][b]Featured characters:[/b] Cao Cao, Yuan Shao, Liu Bei, Sun Jian and Zhang Jiao — or play anyone.
[*][b]Storylines:[/b] the Yellow Turban uprising; the coalition against Dong Zhuo; Liu Bei from volunteer leader to Tao Qian's deathbed bequest of Xu Province; Sun Jian's death at Xian Hill, Sun Ce's crossing into Jiangdong, Sun Quan's succession; Cao Cao's Qingzhou Army; Guandu, Red Cliffs and Jing Province; nine historical revolts from the Black Mountain bandits to Yan Baihu.
[*]Historical warlords rise more readily; the AI offers submission before war, and weak rulers petition strong neighbors for vassalage.
[*]Realm names follow dynasties: Liu becomes Han, Cao becomes Wei, Sun becomes Wu.
[*]Peasant leaders keep raising volunteer armies in war.
[/list]

[h2]■ Hu and Han, 184–600[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]24 historical invasions[/b]: Ke Bineng, Tufa Shujineng, Former Yan, Tuoba Gui, Murong Chui, Yao Chang, Lu Guang, Helian Bobo, Liu Yu, the Six Garrisons, Erzhu Rong, Gao Huan, Yuwen Tai, the Turks, Hou Jing, Yang Jian and more. Each is driven only by the historical figure himself — no stand-ins. If the time is wrong, he waits in his homeland.
[*][b]Submission of the Five Barbarians:[/b] tribes petition to settle or demand land; Liu Yuan, Shi Le, Li Te, Fu Hong and Yao Yizhong can rise as Great Chanyu.
[*][b]Liao Migration into Shu[/b] reshapes the peoples of Ba and Shu.
[*][b]Southern Migration of the Gentry:[/b] Upheaval, Southern Crossing and Stalemate, ending in restoration, sinicization, loss of the north, or lasting division.
[*]Steppe nomads raid south more boldly, especially in times of chaos.
[*]Invasions (one by one), the Five Barbarians, the Liao migration and the southern migration can each be toggled in game rules.
[/list]

[h2]■ FAQ[/h2]
[b]Crashes around 30% loading, or the game won't start?[/b]
Check: ① HanJIn_Clothes is subscribed and enabled; ② you own All Under Heaven and run 1.20.0.4; ③ the beta, the UUII submod and other large mods are off — try this mod plus its requirement alone; ④ verify game files in Steam. If it still fails, send us error.log.

[b]Can I continue an old save?[/b] No, start a new 184 game.

[b]Only one start date?[/b] Only 184 for now. The Three Kingdoms, Jin and the Northern and Southern Dynasties unfold through play.

[b]Compatible with other mods?[/b] Map and history mods are not. Small UI mods — test at your own risk.

[b]Playable without All Under Heaven?[/b] No.
