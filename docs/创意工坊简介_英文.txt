[h1]The Annal of Han-Jin · 汉晋春秋[/h1]

【Cover image: [img]image URL[/img]】

[quote]184 AD, a jiazi year. The Han has ruled for four hundred years. Eunuchs and consort kin feud in the palace, great families swallow the land, and plague and famine drive the people to despair. The Way of Great Peace waits for a single word.
[b]Will you save the tottering Han — or ride the storm and remake the world?[/b][/quote]

[b]The Annal of Han-Jin[/b] is a total conversion devoted to East Asia: a rebuilt map, the complete Han bureaucracy, 30,000+ historical characters, and four centuries of order and chaos, from the Yellow Turbans to the Sui. [b]Fully localized in English.[/b]

[h2]■ The Map and Art[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A large map purely of East Asia[/b], restoring every Han commandery and county: provinces are kingdoms, commanderies duchies, and 2,100+ counties reach from the Pamirs to the steppe, Korea, Wa and Jiaozhi.
[*][b]Completely remade terrain art[/b] — the most detailed East Asian map on the Workshop.
[*][b]40+ unique clothing models and dozens of special building models[/b], plus historical unit models and Chinese coats of arms.
[*][b]Runs at vanilla speed.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]■ The Complete Han Bureaucracy[/h2]
[list]
[*]Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers, shown in a "Hundred Officials" window.
[*]Commanderies and kingdoms, Inspectors raised to Governors, Military Districts with tallies up to the Borrowed Yellow Axe.
[*]Salary ranks from Ten Thousand to Six Hundred Bushels.
[*]Recommendation as Filial and Incorrupt or Flourishing Talent, summons, probation, evaluations, and Virtuous Repute from "Praised in the Village" to "Revered by All Under Heaven".
[*]Open Office with your own staff; military ranks earned by merit.
[*]Inner palace ranks, crown prince and imperial grandson, era names, posthumous and temple names.
[*]184 officeholders follow history wherever possible.
[/list]

[h2]■ Dynastic Cycle: Eight Eras[/h2]
Order: [b]Rebuilding from Ruin, Expansion, Advancement, Tension, Conquest[/b]. Chaos: [b]Collapse of the Realm, Contest of the Warlords, Tripod Standoff[/b].
[list]
[*]Each era has its own decisions and wars: Grand Amnesty, Military Farming, Revise the Statutes, Proscription of Factions, Purge the Emperor's Side, Sinicization Reforms, Edict to Rescue the Throne and more.
[*][b]Collapse:[/b] mass secession, warlords claiming the throne, peasant uprisings, crises of authority, floods, earthquakes and famine.
[*][b]Contest:[/b] vassals Demand Independence, lieges Punish the Rebel, warlords wage Annexation.
[*][b]Standoff:[/b] great powers fight for the Central Plains. Control 80% of the realm to end the chaos.
[*]Regicide, lost wars and failing legitimacy drag the realm down; crushing rebellions pulls it back.
[*]In times of order only the Son of Heaven may create kingdoms and empires. 184 opens in [b]Tension[/b].
[/list]

[h2]■ Emperor and Warlords[/h2]
[list]
[*]A warlord can [b]Hold the Son of Heaven[/b], become Chancellor and rule in his name: Decree a Campaign, Campaign by Edict, Demand Tribute, Grant Remote Claim.
[*]The emperor fights back: Girdle Edict, Secretly Win Over, Flee, Purge the Powerful Minister.
[*]Stay [b]Loyal to Han[/b], or [b]Obey Edicts No More[/b] and Force the Abdication. Usurpers face wars to Punish the Usurper.
[/list]

[h2]■ Court Factions[/h2]
[list]
[*]Consort Kin, Eunuchs, Gentry, Humble-Born and Martial Nobles — plus the Four Barbarians — take stances and clash in court debates.
[*]Celestial dynasties use house blocs and house unity.
[/list]

[h2]■ Characters and Storylines[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]34,000+ characters, 32,000+ historical[/b], with lineages from the sources.
[*][b]11,000+ figures born after 184 arrive at sixteen[/b], year by year, until the end of the Southern and Northern Dynasties.
[*][b]Featured:[/b] Cao Cao, Yuan Shao, Liu Bei, Sun Jian, Zhang Jiao — or anyone.
[*][b]Storylines:[/b] the Yellow Turbans; the coalition against Dong Zhuo; Liu Bei and the bequest of Xu Province; Sun Jian, Sun Ce and Sun Quan in Jiangdong; Cao Cao's Qingzhou Army; Guandu and Red Cliffs; nine historical revolts.
[*]Historical warlords rise more readily; the AI offers submission before war; weak rulers petition for vassalage.
[*]Realm names follow dynasties: Liu is Han, Cao is Wei, Sun is Wu.
[/list]

[h2]■ Hu and Han, 184–600[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]24 historical invasions[/b], from Ke Bineng and Former Yan to Liu Yu, Gao Huan, Hou Jing and Yang Jian — driven only by the historical figure himself, never a stand-in.
[*][b]Submission of the Five Barbarians[/b], with Liu Yuan, Shi Le and others rising as Great Chanyu.
[*][b]Liao Migration into Shu.[/b]
[*][b]Southern Migration of the Gentry:[/b] Upheaval, Southern Crossing, Stalemate, and four possible endings.
[*]Bolder steppe nomads. All of the above can be toggled in game rules.
[/list]

[h2]■ Scholars[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Pure Criticism[/b] gatherings and the [b]Monthly Commentary[/b], where guests vote eight to four to one to crown a renowned scholar.
[*][b]Recluses[/b] who may be summoned to office, and an [b]Imperial Academy[/b] in Luoyang or Chang'an.
[/list]

Adjustable siege devastation; Embattle lieutenants and tactics; the Peach Garden Oath; expanded leagues, schemes and events; all new text in a period voice.

[b]Other start dates?[/b] Only 184; later eras unfold through play.
