[b]The Annal of Han-Jin[/b] is a total conversion mod devoted to East Asia: a redrawn map, the complete Han bureaucracy, and over thirty thousand historical characters, spanning more than four centuries of order and chaos, from the Yellow Turban uprising to Yang Jian supplanting the Northern Zhou.


[h2]■ Highlights[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]A large map focused entirely on East Asia[/b]: every Han commandery and county restored, with completely remade terrain art.
[*][b]40+ unique clothing models and dozens of special building models.[/b]
[*][b]The complete Han bureaucracy[/b]: the Three Excellencies and Nine Ministers, commanderies, provinces and counties, salary ranks, recommendation and summons, area commanders and imperial tallies, the inner palace and the imperial house.
[*][b]An eight-era Dynastic Cycle[/b]: five eras of order, three of chaos. In times of chaos, you can hold the Son of Heaven and command the lords in his name.
[*][b]Court factions[/b]: Consort Kin, Eunuchs, Gentry, Humble-Born and Martial Nobles vie for power.
[*][b]30,000+ historical characters, 11,000+ of them arriving year by year[/b], with historical storylines: the Yellow Turbans, the coalition against Dong Zhuo, Liu Bei in Xu Province, the Sun family in Jiangdong, Cao Cao's Qingzhou Army, and more.
[*][b]Hu and Han, 184–600[/b]: 24 historical invasions, the Submission of the Five Barbarians, the Liao Migration into Shu, and the Southern Migration of the Gentry.
[*][b]Performance on par with vanilla.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]■ Other Changes[/h2]

[*]Sieges: devastation, epidemics, building destruction, encampments and sacking, all adjustable in game rules.

[h2]■ FAQ[/h2]

[b]Q: Is 184 the only start date?[/b]
A: For now, yes. The Three Kingdoms, Jin and the Northern and Southern Dynasties unfold naturally as you play, and historical figures arrive year by year. We will gradually add Wei-Jin and Northern and Southern Dynasties start dates up to the early Sui, and then scenarios such as the Xin dynasty and the Eastern Han.

[b]Q: Is it compatible with other mods?[/b]
A: This mod replaces the map, titles, characters and history, so any mod that changes the map or history is incompatible. Small UI-only mods may work; please test them yourself.
